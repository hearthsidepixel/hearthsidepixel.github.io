GIFT IDEAS

Gift Ideas for a Grandma Who Loves Coloring and Puzzles

Choosing a gift for a grandmother who already has a lot can be hard. If she likes coloring, puzzles or quiet hobbies, a few simple ideas can make the gift feel personal. Here is a short list you can mix and match.

1. A color by number book she can finish page by page

A book gives her something to look forward to every evening. Check that the squares are big enough to see comfortably and that the color key sits next to the grid. (Disclosure: we make color by number books, including Cozy Christmas Color by Number, so judge for yourself with the free pages on this site.)

2. A set of good markers or pencils

Thick-barrel pencils or soft markers are easier on the hands. A set of 24 colors covers most books. See our supplies guide.

3. A lap desk or tray

A flat, steady surface for the sofa or an armchair makes coloring much more comfortable.

4. A daylight lamp

Good light is kind to eyes, and it makes small codes easier to read.

5. A cozy extras bundle

Pair the book with a mug, loose tea or hot cocoa, and a soft blanket. It turns a book into a full "quiet evening" gift.

6. Something she can do with you

Print two copies of a free page and color it together over the phone or at the table. Many families find this is the part of the gift they remember.
