How it works

Download & print

Open any page, press Download PDF and print it in black and white. Letter or A4 both work.

Match the codes

Each number or letter has a color in the key. Fill every square with its color, one color at a time.

Reveal the picture

The picture appears as you go. Page 2 of the PDF shows the finished answer in color.

Easy pages for beginners

Fewer squares, fewer colors, about 20–30 minutes each.
